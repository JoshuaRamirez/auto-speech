The calibration measurement revealed that Kokoro synthesizes roughly sixteen characters per second at the default voice and speed. That figure anchors every decision the chunk planner will make. Consider a response of around two thousand characters. Divided by sixteen characters per second, that response will take roughly two minutes to speak aloud. Under a Fibonacci schedule with a base of four seconds, the first chunk runs for about four seconds. The second runs for four more. The third for eight, the fourth for twelve, the fifth for twenty, the sixth for thirty-two. After just six chunks we have covered eighty seconds of audio. The seventh chunk adds fifty-two seconds more, comfortably reaching the full transcript. Generation stays ahead of playback the whole time, because Kokoro runs many times faster than realtime on this machine.

The measurement of time in human speech is surprisingly consistent. When a careful reader delivers a paragraph of ordinary English prose, the tempo falls into a narrow band. This reference passage exists to serve as a calibration target for a speech system. Its purpose is modest. It contains no numbers that need expanding, no acronyms that need spelling out, and no code that needs translating. It is just a paragraph. A person could read it in an unhurried voice, pausing at every period, resting briefly at every comma, and feeling no rush to finish. That is exactly how the system should behave.
